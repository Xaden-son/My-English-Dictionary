## Abundant

Present in large quantities. 

**Example:** Living close to a lake means we have an abundant supply of water.

## Accumulate

Means to collect or gather something over time. 

**Example:** Dust accumulates on the shelves if you don’t clean them.

## Accurate

Correct, exact, or without mistakes.

## Accustomed

Being used to something because it happens often or is familiar. 

**Example:** She is accustomed to waking up early.

## Acquire

To get or gain something, especially through effort or experience. 

**Example:** She acquired a new skill in coding. / He acquired a rare book for his collection.

## Adamant

Refusing to change an opinion. 

**Example:** He is adamant that he is right.

## Adequate

Enough to suit your needs. 

**Example:** Our house isn’t big, but it’s adequate for the two of us.

## Adjacent

Very near, next to, or touching. 

**Example:** The park is adjacent to the school.

## Adjust(v)

To change something slightly to make it better or more suitable. 

**Example:** He adjusted his chair to sit more comfortably.

## Adjustment(n)

A small change.

## Advocate

To support or speak in favor of something. 

**Example:** She advocates for animal rights.

## Favor

It can mean a kind act, support, or preference for something. 

**Example:** Can you do me a favor? / She is in favor of the new rule. / The teacher favors hardworking students.

## Adverse

It means harmful, negative, or unfavorable. 

**Example:** Smoking has adverse effects on health.

To wander away from a place or person, or something that is lost or not where it should be.
